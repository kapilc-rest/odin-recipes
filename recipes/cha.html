Cha Recipe

Description

Cha is the de facto national beverage of India. The cold winter or scorching summer, tea is always consumed. Without tea, the day doesn't seem to have started or ended. Wanna have a good day, Have a cup of tea!

There are different varieties of Tea including laal cha, green tea, masala chai. In this page, we will be going through milk tea, which is a toned-down version of masala chai.

Ingredients

  • Cha-paati
  • 1 glass of water
  • 1 teaspoon of sugar
  • Milk powder

Steps

  1. Pour a glass of water into a saucepan and heat it for a minute.
  2. Add a palm full of cha-patti until and let it boil for a minute.
  3. Pour the tea into a glass.
  4. Mix milk powder and sugar with it.
  5. Finally, Serve with biscuits or muri.
