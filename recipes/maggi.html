Boiled Maggi Recipe

Description

Maggi, Maggi, Maggi!

Ingredients

  • One 10rs maggi packet
  • 50ml of water

Steps

  1. Put 50ml to a electric cattle.
  2. Scruch the maggi packet a bit.
  3. Cut the packet and pour maggi into the water.
  4. Close the lid and let it boil until it turns off.
  5. Pour the boiled maggi along with water into a bowl.
  6. Pour the masala that's given with the packet.
  7. Stir a bit to mix with the maggi and eat it with hand!
